# Your Privacy Choices

Sai ki Family gives you choices about the information you share.

## Profile and content

Open your profile to review the details attached to your account. You can edit or remove supported experiences, conversations, listings, and events through their available controls. Content already copied or shared by others may remain outside our service.

## Device permissions

Use your phone's Settings to change Sai ki Family's microphone, photos, location, and notification permissions. Turning off a permission may stop the feature that needs it.

## Analytics

We use Mixpanel to understand app usage. Analytics events can be associated with your account ID. Contact vikasahuja1971@gmail.com if you have questions about analytics data associated with your account.

## Access, correction, and deletion

To ask about a copy of your information, corrections, or deletion, email vikasahuja1971@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or include your account ID and a safe way to verify ownership. Do not send your password or OTP. We will explain any information we must retain under applicable law.
