# Sai ki Family

Faith, connection, and service in one community for Sai devotees.

Share experiences, temple visits, and meaningful moments. Discover devotional events and communities. Connect with fellow devotees, explore community business listings, and keep count of your Naam Jaap practice.

Ask Sai offers AI-generated guidance in English and Hindi through text and voice. Its replies draw on Sai Baba's values and may reference Shri Sai Satcharitra when supported by available material. Ask Sai is an AI assistant, not Sai Baba, and can make mistakes. It is not a source of medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice.

## Explore

- Experiences: share posts, photos, videos, and audio with the community.
- Events: find gatherings, create an event, and manage your attendance.
- Sai Family: discover people, groups, conversations, and community businesses.
- Naam Jaap: count repetitions and follow your personal practice.
- Ask Sai: write or speak a question and receive AI-generated guidance.
